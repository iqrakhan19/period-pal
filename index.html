Eating well around your period

Nourish your body with balanced meals and foods that help support your wellbeing.

Can food control my flow?

No diet reliably makes a period lighter or heavier. Your flow is mostly shaped by hormones, and conditions such as thyroid problems, PCOS, fibroids, stress, weight changes, or contraception can affect it too. Eating balanced meals can help you feel steadier and replace nutrients such as iron.

🫘 Lentils and beans Dal, rajma, and chickpeas provide iron and protein.
🥬 Leafy greens Spinach, methi, and amaranth contain iron and folate.
🍊 Vitamin C foods Orange, lemon, amla, and guava help your body absorb plant iron.
🥜 Seeds and nuts Pumpkin seeds and almonds provide useful nutrients.
🍇 Dates and raisins A small serving can contribute some iron.
🥚 Eggs, fish, or chicken Options for those who include animal foods in their diet.
🌾 Whole grains Ragi, oats, and brown rice provide energy and nutrients.
💧 Water Keep sipping throughout the day.
  • Pair iron-rich plant foods with vitamin C foods.
  • Tea and coffee can reduce iron absorption when consumed with meals.
  • Avoid skipping meals or following extreme diets.
  • Ask a healthcare professional before using products that promise to regulate your period.
When to seek medical advice: If you regularly soak through a pad or tampon every 1–2 hours, bleeding lasts longer than 7 days, you feel dizzy or breathless, or your pain is severe, contact a healthcare professional. Heavy periods are common and treatable.
